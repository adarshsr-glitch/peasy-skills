Let users log in with itsme, Google and Apple

Summary

Peasy login is email + password only. Belgian users expect to sign in with itsme, Google or Apple. This epic adds those three providers, links them to existing accounts and pre-fills the profile from itsme so insurance and mortgage quotes need fewer fields.

Context

  • itsme is Belgium's national digital ID app (eIDAS high), widely used for banking and government services.
  • Insurance and mortgage quotes ask for name, address and date of birth; itsme can supply these verified.
  • Current behaviour: email + password with "Forgot password", no social login.
  • itsme has 8M+ users in Belgium and is eIDAS-notified at assurance level high.

Scope

In scope

  • Sign up / log in with itsme, Google, Apple (web)
  • Auto-link to existing account on verified matching email
  • Profile pre-fill from itsme (name, address, date of birth)
  • GDPR consent screen for itsme data (nl-BE)

Out of scope

  • Hypotheek.winkel, Verzekeringen.be, Aanbieders.be (phase 2)
  • Mobile app
  • Removing email + password
  • itsme e-signature for contracts

User stories

S1 Log in with itsme

5 pts

As a Belgian user, I want to log in with itsme, so that I don't need another password.

Given I'm on the login page When I choose "Inloggen met itsme" and approve in the app Then I'm logged in and land on my dashboard.
Given I cancel in the itsme app When I return to Peasy Then I see "Inloggen geannuleerd. Probeer opnieuw of kies een andere manier." and no account is created.
Given itsme is unavailable When I try to log in Then I see an error banner and the other login options stay usable.

S2 Log in with Google or Apple

3 pts

As a user, I want to log in with Google or Apple, so that signing up takes one click.

Given I choose Google or Apple When the provider returns a verified email Then an account is created or I'm logged in.
Given Apple returns a private relay email When the account is created Then we store the relay address and don't ask for the real one.

S3 Link to my existing account

5 pts

As an existing email user, I want my new login method linked to my account, so that I keep my comparisons and contracts.

Given a provider returns a verified email matching an existing account When I log in Then the provider is linked and I see a confirmation.
Given the email is not verified by the provider When I log in Then we ask for my Peasy password before linking.

S4 Pre-fill my profile from itsme

3 pts

As a user requesting an insurance or mortgage quote, I want my details filled from itsme, so that I type less and avoid mistakes.

Given I log in with itsme for the first time When I give consent Then name, address and date of birth are shown pre-filled and editable.
Given my itsme data differs from my saved profile When I log in Then I see the differences and choose which to keep.
Given I decline consent When I continue Then I'm logged in without pre-fill.

S5 Manage login methods

2 pts

As a user, I want to see and unlink my login methods, so that I stay in control of my account.

Given I have two or more login methods When I unlink one Then it's removed and I get a confirmation email.
Given I have only one login method When I try to unlink it Then the action is disabled with an explanation.

Non-functional

  • Privacy: consent-based pre-fill; don't store the national register number; log consent with timestamp; update privacy policy (controller Verzekeringen NV). [Legal text: todo]
  • Security: OIDC with PKCE; state + nonce checks; session length unchanged.
  • Accessibility: WCAG 2.2 AA; provider buttons keyboard-reachable with visible focus.
  • Analytics: login_method_selected, login_success, login_failed, account_linked, prefill_consent.

Dependencies

  • itsme partner contract and test environment
  • Apple developer account: Sign in with Apple service ID
  • Legal review of consent wording (NL + FR)

Assumptions

  • Assumption: Google and Apple emails are treated as verified.
  • Assumption: Existing auth service supports multiple identities per user.
